About Wings Academy

Better Teachers.
Brighter Futures.

Located on Tounsa Street, Garden Town (opposite the PPSC Office), Wings Academy Multan is a trusted evening coaching centre for secondary and higher-secondary students. We prepare students for both Punjab Board and Federal Board (HSSC-I & II) exams.

Our approach is simple: clear concepts, consistent practice and honest feedback. Through regular test sessions that mirror the real exam, students build the confidence to perform on the day that matters.

  • Concept-based teaching
  • Notes, worksheets & past papers
  • Regular tests & performance tracking
  • Individual attention & career guidance
  • Separate classes for boys & girls
  • Disciplined learning environment
